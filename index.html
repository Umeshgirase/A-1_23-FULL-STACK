About KBTCOE Nashik

Building Engineers for Tomorrow

Karmaveer Adv. Baburao Ganpatrao Thakare College of Engineering (KBTCOE) is committed to providing quality technical education and developing skilled, innovative and responsible engineers.

The college provides students with a learning environment supported by experienced faculty, modern laboratories, academic resources and opportunities for professional development.
